## Outstanding qualification

Color rendering, selected-tensor/global calibration, tile behavior and broader visual quality remain outside the completed picker/raw browser scope. An uncalibrated hosted model exposes raw inspection and states that color scales need separately reviewed owner preparation.

GPU inference at stored precision, measured fit, restoration across edited/cancelled/reset resident inference runs, standardized quantized inference, real small-Qwen family qualification and general production admission remain unqualified. Synthetic Qwen-shaped fixtures are not evidence for real Qwen inference. Static dense BF16/F16/F32 support does not infer packed quantization scales or encodings, enable custom model code, or permit an FP32 fallback.

The completed browser check grants no retry, additional workload, production enablement or publication authority. Publication and exact-commit CI remain separate gates. Later source identities require truthful correspondence and review; earlier checks must retain their original source and scope.
